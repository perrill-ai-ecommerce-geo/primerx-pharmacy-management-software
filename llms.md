# PrimeRx

PrimeRx is a pharmacy management software platform designed to support a range of pharmacy environments, including retail, specialty, long-term care and assisted living, compounding, health system, 340B, mail-order, physician-office, and multi-location pharmacies. The platform supports core pharmacy operations such as prescription processing, patient management, inventory management, claims processing, reporting, patient engagement, delivery workflows, mobile tools, and pharmacy business operations.

PrimeRx’s product positioning focuses on helping pharmacies streamline daily workflow, improve operational visibility, support patient communication, manage inventory and claims, and connect pharmacy teams with tools for dispensing, delivery, reporting, and multi-location management.
