# PrimeRx — Pharmacy Management Software

PrimeRx is pharmacy management software designed for independent retail pharmacies and other pharmacy settings, including specialty, long-term care, compounding, health system, 340B, mail-order, and physician-office pharmacies. It helps streamline daily workflows, support patient care, improve visibility across pharmacy operations, and connect pharmacy teams with tools for prescription processing, inventory, claims, reporting, patient engagement, delivery, and business management.

## Key capabilities

- **Prescription workflows:** Tools for prescription processing, refill management, workflow organization, labeling, and dispensing.
- **Patient management:** Patient profiles, medication histories, notes, communication tools, and patient care information.
- **Clinical services:** Support for pharmacy care workflows, immunization records, care plans, assessments, and eCare Plan capabilities.
- **Inventory controls:** Inventory tracking, stock adjustments, purchasing support, physical inventory tools, and reporting to help pharmacies manage medication supply.
- **Billing & claims:** Support for prescription claim submission, adjudication workflows, rejection management, and other pharmacy billing processes.
- **Reports & insights:** Operational, prescription, inventory, financial, and business reporting to help pharmacy owners monitor performance.
- **Patient engagement:** Tools such as two-way SMS, email communication, refill reminders, and FillMyRefills for online prescription refill requests.
- **Delivery management:** PrimeDELIVERY supports pharmacy delivery workflows, including routing, signature capture, and payment collection.
- **Mobile tools:** PrimeRx offers mobile tools such as PrimeINVENTORY and the MyPrimeRx Owners App to support inventory management and operational visibility.
- **Multi-store management:** The PrimeRx Central Management Portal helps pharmacy organizations manage reporting and operations across multiple locations.
